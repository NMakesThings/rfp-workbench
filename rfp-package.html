Deliverable Builder Help

Use this page to assemble reviewed workbench content into draft/readiness outputs.

  • Deliverable Builder summarizes readiness and exports outline-level Markdown only.
  • It does not generate final client-ready DOCX or PDF files.
  • Upstream pages provide context, source material, analysis, templates, and reviewed records.
  • Requirements Review owns Requirements Matrix CSV and review brief outputs.
  • Client templates guide structure and insertion areas but are not populated automatically.
